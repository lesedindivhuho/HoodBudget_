The gamification elements that were added to the Hood budget app are:
1. Monthly goals section is the strongest gamification feature the users set a minimum and maximum spending goal. This creates a challenge or target for the user to achieve.
2. Progress tracking. Real-time budget statistics provide continuous feedback, motivating users to monitor and improve their spending habits. 
3. Achievement Badges were added as a card below the hero banner. Visual rewards users earn when they complete specific actions, hit milestones, or demonstrate consistent engagement within the app.
4. Daily Login Streaks were added near the top of the Dashboard. A daily login streak tracks how many consecutive days the user logs into the system without breaking the chain.
3. The graph and pie chart were added showing the amount spent per category over a user-selectable period.
